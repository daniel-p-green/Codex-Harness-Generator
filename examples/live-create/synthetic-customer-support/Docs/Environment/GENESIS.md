# Genesis

## Project Summary

Synthetic customer-support workspace for public-safe grounded support-note
examples. The example uses fake ticket and policy excerpts seeded during task
trials.

## User Context

- Experience level: intermediate
- Work-area shape: one focused area
- Generation mode: public-safe high-risk task-trial fixture
- Data sensitivity: synthetic only
- External services: none requested

## Work Types

- Inspect local synthetic support tickets, policy notes, and product notes.
- Write source-backed FAQ, response, or escalation notes.
- Separate grounded claims, `[VERIFY]` gaps, `[PROPOSED]` commitments, privacy
  checks, escalation triggers, and human-review limits.
- Recommend human owner, legal, PR, security, privacy, or compliance review when
  the request becomes approved customer-facing policy or regulated handling.

## Constraints

- This is draft support documentation, not approved customer-facing policy.
- Do not invent product facts, refund terms, SLAs, eligibility, timelines,
  fixes, account commitments, or legal positions.
- Do not include real customer data, personal data, PII, account identifiers,
  payment data, health data, or confidential support transcripts in public
  artifacts.
