# Core Rules

Use routing to decide whether to inspect source files, ask for jurisdiction, or invoke the reviewer.

Autonomy: make low-risk local reads and edits, but request clarification before legal advice, strategy, filing, or real-world decision support.

Context: preserve source paths, section labels, jurisdiction, assumptions, and verification status.

Error handling: fail loud when citations are missing, jurisdiction is unclear, facts are private, or attorney judgment is required.

Self-learning: write retro notes for repeated citation or boundary issues and update the harness after validated patterns emerge.

Self-learning: record repeated friction and user corrections in `Docs/Environment/IMPROVEMENT_LOG.md` before updating harness behavior.
