# Genesis

## Project Summary

Synthetic legal research workspace for public-safe source-fidelity examples. The
example uses fake policy excerpts seeded during task trials.

## User Context

- Experience level: intermediate
- Work-area shape: one focused area
- Generation mode: public-safe high-risk task-trial fixture
- Data sensitivity: synthetic only
- External services: none requested

## Work Types

- Inspect local synthetic legal or policy excerpts.
- Write source-backed research notes.
- Separate jurisdiction, source-backed conclusions, assumptions, uncertainty,
  and limits.
- Recommend attorney review when the request becomes advice or strategy.

## Constraints

- This is research support, not legal advice.
- Do not invent statutes, cases, deadlines, parties, or standards.
- Do not include personal data, client names, privileged facts, or confidential
  matter details in public artifacts.
