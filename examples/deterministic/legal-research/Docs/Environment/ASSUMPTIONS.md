# Assumptions

- Assumption: This deterministic harness targets local legal or policy research artifacts.
- Assumption: Legal claims require source citations and clear limits.
- Assumption: Verification means checking jurisdiction, source citations, assumptions, uncertainty, and attorney-review needs.
- Limit: It is a minimal acceptance harness, not a full model-mediated custom `/create` run.
- Verify: Run `python scripts/run-harness-evals.py` locally, or run `codex-harness validate <target>` from the generator repo.
