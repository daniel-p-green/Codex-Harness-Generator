# Genesis

## Project Summary

Synthetic hiring-pipeline workspace for public-safe structured scorecard
examples. The example uses fake role requirements and candidate evidence seeded
during task trials.

## User Context

- Experience level: intermediate
- Work-area shape: one focused area
- Generation mode: public-safe high-risk task-trial fixture
- Data sensitivity: synthetic only
- External services: none requested

## Work Types

- Inspect local synthetic role and candidate-evidence notes.
- Write source-backed structured scorecard notes.
- Separate role requirements, criteria, evidence, bias checks, privacy checks,
  and human-review limits.
- Recommend human recruiter, hiring manager, HR, or counsel review when the
  request becomes a real candidate decision, adverse action, or legal judgment.

## Constraints

- This is hiring decision support, not a hire/no-hire verdict or legal advice.
- Do not invent candidate evidence, protected-class traits, rankings, rejection
  reasons, or compliance conclusions.
- Do not include real candidate data, personal data, PII, contact details, or
  confidential hiring records in public artifacts.
